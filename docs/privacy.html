Face Gallery Privacy Policy

Updated: 10 October 2026
Developer: Ashwini Dhatterwal

Your media and people groups

Face Gallery displays the photos and videos you allow it to access. Videos play locally; only photos are submitted for face recognition. Face detection and recognition run on your device. Photo content and face recognition outputs are not sent to the developer or to a cloud recognition service. The app stores face locations, numerical face signatures, group membership, names you enter, manual corrections, tags, optional place labels you enter, favourites and settings in its private app storage. Face grouping can make mistakes; you can correct assignments. It is not identity verification or a security feature.

Permissions

Photo access is used to browse, edit and organise photos, including background recognition. Video access is used for local browsing, thumbnails and playback. On supported Android versions, you can limit access to selected photos and videos. Contacts access is optional: it lets you search contacts, display contact photos and link a people group to a contact. With automatic contact matching enabled, the app also recognises suitable contact portraits locally and attaches clear matches to unnamed gallery groups. Missing or unusable portraits leave grouping unchanged. Similar but uncertain faces can suggest contact names or manually named groups; suggestions require your confirmation. You can request a contact-only check with Sync contact photos without changing automatic processing settings. User names and corrections take priority. Contact portrait signatures, failed/skipped processing status and change fingerprints are cached in private app storage so unchanged photos are not repeatedly recognised. No contact phone numbers or email addresses are needed for this feature. Turn off Match contact photos automatically in Settings > Recognition and contacts to remove the portrait signature cache and stop automatic contact naming; existing group names and contact links remain editable. When you manually link a contact without a photo, you may choose a clear gallery face crop as its contact photo. Write contacts permission is requested only after you accept this action. Existing contact photos are preserved. Photos written to your contacts remain there after clearing app data and may sync through your contacts account under its own policy. You can type a name without giving contacts permission. The app stores the selected contact lookup link and accesses permitted contact names and photos locally; it does not upload your address book. Notification permission supports scan status and pause controls. Background processing may use scheduled jobs and a temporary wake lock; it pauses for battery or temperature constraints.

Background work and control

Before enabling automatic people recognition, the app explains its photo and optional contact use and asks for your affirmative choice. Browse only leaves automatic recognition and contact matching off. Android permissions are requested afterwards; granting permissions alone does not authorize automatic processing. Existing installations also receive this explanation before automatic processing resumes. Recognition can continue after you close the gallery. Automatic processing uses scheduled jobs with no progress notification. A scan explicitly started from Recognition tools uses a foreground service with a status notification and a Pause for 24 hours action. Turn off Background recognition in Settings > Recognition and contacts to pause automatic work even when no notification is visible. Notification pauses save progress and automatically retry after 24 hours when Android schedules work and battery/temperature allow it. The Background recognition switch stays off until you turn it on again. You can also pause a running foreground scan from Recognition tools. Revoking photo permission stops access; revoking permission does not by itself erase previously saved groups.

Sharing and exports

Photos and videos are sent to another app only when you use Android's share interface. That app's handling is covered by its own policy. Edited copies are saved to your photo storage; original photos are retained by editing. A diagnostic report is exported only when you choose that action. It contains local photo identifiers, group identifiers, status, scores and counts, plus contact processing stages, pseudonymous contact tags and cached portrait quality measurements. It does not contain photo pixels, face vectors, contact names, phone numbers, email addresses or contact lookup links. The Contact diagnostics screen displays permitted contact names locally to help you find a failed portrait; exported reports omit those names. Reports also include bounded local People loading failure types and code locations, without exception messages, tags or saved place labels. An exported file can remain in your chosen location after uninstalling Face Gallery.

Third-party face detection

This app includes Google's bundled ML Kit face detector and the LiteRT runtime. Google's documentation states that image inputs and recognition outputs are processed on-device. Google also documents technical SDK metrics such as device and app information, installation identifiers, API events, performance and errors, used to maintain and improve its APIs. Those metrics do not contain your photo content or face recognition output. Face Gallery has no Internet permission, no developer analytics server, no advertisements and no user account. Network permission removal alone is not presented as proof that all third-party SDK diagnostics are disabled. Google's ML Kit terms and privacy information are available at developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms and policies.google.com/privacy.

Retention and deletion

Local recognition results remain until you clear face results, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Clear results in Settings > Recognition tools removes saved detection, signatures, contact portrait caches, people names, contact links and grouping corrections; recognition can recreate results while it is enabled. Turn off Background recognition first if you do not want results recreated. Clear all app storage in Android Settings to remove the app's settings, favourites, tags and saved place labels as well. App-managed cloud backup is disabled. Your device photos, saved edits, externally exported reports and files you shared remain under your control and are not deleted by clearing app storage.
