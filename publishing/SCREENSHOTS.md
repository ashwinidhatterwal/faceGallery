# Store capture plan

Capture genuine screens from the signed release with an owned/consented sample library. Minimum two screenshots; four recommended at 1080 × 1920, portrait 9:16. Use JPEG or RGB PNG without alpha. Do not stretch a tall screenshot to 9:16; capture a suitable device/emulator or crop without changing the UI. Keep within Play's dimensions and maximum 2:1 aspect-ratio requirement.

Suggested order: (1) mixed Photos grid with photo/video badges; (2) People/Search with consented named groups; (3) a photo's people panel; (4) video playback and seek controls. Avoid contact phone numbers, emails, private names, notifications and other personal data. Use permission to show identifiable faces.

Existing `design/review` images are UI regression evidence, not a complete store screenshot set. Feature graphic artwork is promotional branding, not an app screenshot. An optional preview video is separate from the required foreground-service demonstration.
