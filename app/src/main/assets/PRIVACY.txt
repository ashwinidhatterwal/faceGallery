Mosaic Gallery Privacy Policy
Updated: 8 October 2026
Developer: Ashwini Dhatterwal

Your photos and people groups
Mosaic Gallery displays the photos you allow it to access. Face detection and recognition run on your device. Photo content and face recognition outputs are not sent to the developer or to a cloud recognition service. The app stores face locations, numerical face signatures, group membership, names you enter, manual corrections, favourites and settings in its private app storage. Face grouping can make mistakes; you can correct assignments. It is not identity verification or a security feature.

Permissions
Photo access is used to browse, edit and organise your library, including background recognition. On supported Android versions, you can limit access to selected photos. Contacts access is optional: it lets you search contacts, display contact photos and link a people group to a contact. With automatic contact matching enabled, the app also recognises suitable contact portraits locally and attaches clear matches to unnamed gallery groups. Missing, unclear or ambiguous portraits leave existing grouping unchanged. User names and corrections take priority. Contact portrait signatures, failed/skipped processing status and change fingerprints are cached in private app storage so unchanged photos are not repeatedly recognised. No contact phone numbers or email addresses are needed for this feature. Turn off Match contact photos automatically in Settings > Privacy to remove the portrait signature cache and stop automatic contact naming; existing group names and contact links remain editable. You can type a name without giving contacts permission. The app stores the selected contact lookup link and accesses permitted contact names and photos locally; it does not upload your address book. Notification permission supports scan status and pause controls. Background processing may use scheduled jobs and a temporary wake lock; it pauses for battery or temperature constraints.

Background work and control
Recognition can continue after you close the gallery. Automatic foreground work shows a quiet status with a Pause action, while scheduled jobs have no progress notification. Turn off Background recognition in Settings > Privacy to pause automatic work even when no notification is visible. You can also pause a running foreground scan from its status or Settings > Advanced. Revoking photo permission stops access; revoking permission does not by itself erase previously saved groups.

Sharing and exports
Photos are sent to another app only when you use Android's share interface. That app's handling is covered by its own policy. Edited copies are saved to your photo storage; original photos are retained by editing. A diagnostic report is exported only when you choose that action. It contains local photo identifiers, group identifiers, status, scores and counts; it does not contain photo pixels, face vectors, names or contact links. An exported file can remain in your chosen location after uninstalling Mosaic Gallery.

Third-party face detection
This app includes Google's bundled ML Kit face detector and the LiteRT runtime. Google's documentation states that image inputs and recognition outputs are processed on-device. Google also documents technical SDK metrics such as device and app information, installation identifiers, API events, performance and errors, used to maintain and improve its APIs. Those metrics do not contain your photo content or face recognition output. Mosaic Gallery has no Internet permission, no developer analytics server, no advertisements and no user account. Network permission removal alone is not presented as proof that all third-party SDK diagnostics are disabled. Google's ML Kit terms and privacy information are available at developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms and policies.google.com/privacy.

Retention and deletion
Local recognition results remain until you clear face results, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Clear results in Settings > Advanced removes saved detection, signatures, contact portrait caches, people names, contact links and grouping corrections; recognition can recreate results while it is enabled. Turn off Background recognition first if you do not want results recreated. Clear all app storage in Android Settings to remove the app's settings and favourites as well. App-managed cloud backup is disabled. Your device photos, saved edits, externally exported reports and files you shared remain under your control and are not deleted by clearing app storage.

Contact
For support or privacy questions, use the developer contact published on Mosaic Gallery's Google Play listing.
